How BharatLink AI Procurement Works

Learn how our 6-agent LangGraph workflow evaluates 444+ GI-certified Indian artisan products, verifies stock inventory, maps ASCII transit corridors, and assesses real-time weather risks for institutional buyers.

1

Agent 1: Buyer Intent Parser

Analyzes natural language buyer prompts (e.g. "Source 50 Nauvari Paithani Sarees for London export in 10 days"). Extracts structured sourcing parameters including target product noun, quantity requested, delivery city, budget ceiling, and target lead-time.

2

Agent 2: Discovery Agent & Groq GPT-OSS-20B Search

Invokes Groq's dedicated openai/gpt-oss-20b LLM model and Semantic Core Noun Scoring engine across our 444+ verified Supabase artisan products catalog. Enforces strict zero-hallucination grounding—if a product does not exist in inventory, it outputs an explicit catalog status rather than suggesting unrelated items.

3

Agent 3: Procurement Evaluation & Multi-Seller Comparison

Evaluates candidate artisan guilds for active stock inventory, minimum order quantities (MOQ), GI-tag certification authenticity, seller ratings, and location proximity. Ranks all candidate sellers offering the target product in an itemized comparison matrix.

4

Agent 4: Visual Transit Corridor & Route Optimizer

Maps the logistics corridor from the artisan origin hub to the destination city using clear visual ASCII transit route diagrams (e.g. [ 📍 Origin ] ──────► [ 🔄 Hub ] ──────► [ 🏁 Destination ]).

5

Agent 5: Risk & Live Weather Assessment

Fetches real-time Open-Meteo weather metrics for origin and destination cities to assess climate safety, precipitation risks, and transit feasibility.

6

Agent 6: Executive Synthesis & Sourcing Plan

Synthesizes the complete multi-agent workflow into an executive sourcing plan with product inventory breakdown, seller contact integration, and PDF recommendation export.
