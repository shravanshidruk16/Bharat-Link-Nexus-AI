Artisan Upliftment & Pricing Growth Strategies

Actionable Guidelines to Scale Your Craft Business & Maximize Profit

💡 Optimal Wholesale Pricing Strategy

  • Cost-Plus Craft Margin: Add a minimum 35–45% margin above raw material and artisan weaving wages.
  • Tiered Quantity Pricing: Offer 5–10% bulk discount for orders exceeding 100 units to attract hotel & wedding buyers.
  • Transparent Freight Inclusion: Clearly specify whether shipping cost is included or extra per unit.

🛡️ Leveraging GI Tag Certification

  • Premium Pricing Power: GI Certified authentic items command 20–30% higher export prices globally.
  • Buyer Trust: Display your official GI registration badge in product descriptions for instant buyer verification.
  • Government Export Subsidies: Certified GI guilds qualify for government trade fair grants.

📦 Product Packaging & Export Standards

  • Moisture-Proof Wrapping: Use eco-friendly vacuum sealed bags for silk textiles & woodcrafts during transit.
  • Barcode & Story Labeling: Attach a small card telling the story of your weaver guild and artisan heritage.
  • Prompt WhatsApp Communication: Respond to buyer WhatsApp inquiries within 15 minutes to secure orders.
