Frequently Asked Buyer Questions

What is BharatLink Nexus AI?

BharatLink Nexus AI is an intelligent B2B artisan procurement and freight logistics platform. It uses 6 specialized LangGraph AI agents and Groq's openai/gpt-oss-20b model to discover authentic Indian handicraft suppliers, verify stock inventory, and map route lead times for institutional buyers.

How does Geographical Indication (GI) verification work?

GI tags certify products possessing unique qualities originating from specific geographical locations in India. BharatLink verifies artisan seller credentials and craft registry certifications for items like Paithani Sarees, Kashmiri Pashmina, and Jaipur Blue Pottery.

How are freight shipping charges calculated?

BharatLink displays a visual ASCII transit route corridor mapping origin artisan hubs to destination cities. Final dispatch terms and delivery modes are confirmed directly with the primary seller.
