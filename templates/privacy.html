Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • BharatLink Nexus AI

1. Information We Collect

BharatLink Nexus AI collects minimal required procurement and business credentials to facilitate B2B artisan sourcing:

  • Buyer Account Information: Full name, institutional email address, company name, and country location.
  • Seller Central Registration: Business name, contact person, phone number, WhatsApp dispatch number, state, city, and craft category.
  • Procurement Requests: Sourcing prompts, requested product types, quantity, and destination location.

2. How Data Is Used

We use collected data strictly to execute autonomous multi-agent procurement workflows, match buyers with verified GI artisan collectives, optimize transit routes, and support direct seller communications. We do NOT sell user data or private procurement records to third parties.

3. Data Protection & Search Indexing Rules

Private buyer dashboards, saved sourcing histories, seller management portals, and API payloads are strictly configured with noindex, nofollow directives and authentication barriers. Search engines are blocked from accessing private procurement data.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy or data retention, contact us at privacy@bharatlinknexus.ai.
